A resort you own, run like a hotel.

Serene Heights is a hotel and resort on the main Nathia Gali road, fifty metres from the PAF airbase. Every apartment is furnished, serviced and let to guests by DM Consortium as one hotel.

Owners pay no maintenance charges and no hidden costs. They receive a share of hotel revenue every quarter and 28 nights a year to stay free, in winter or summer.
